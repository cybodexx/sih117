## Bearing Defects

### 1. Spalled bearing race close-up
Extreme macro photograph of a deep groove ball bearing race removed from a steam turbine
drive-end. The inner race surface shows a 12mm oval spalling patch with rough pitted texture
and dark oxidation around the crater edge. Harsh overhead industrial LED lighting, shallow
depth of field, background blurred showing a blue metal workbench with scattered tools. The
bearing still has a thin film of dark degraded lubricant. Shot on a 100mm macro lens,
800 ISO, f/5.6.

### 2. Overheated bearing with discolouration
Photograph of a large roller bearing assembly sitting on a stainless steel inspection tray.
The outer race has a prominent blue-purple heat discolouration band spanning approximately
60 degrees of arc. Thermographic overlay inset showing a 142°C hotspot on the race. The
grease around the damaged area is blackened and carbonised. Overhead fluorescent lighting,
clean room environment, white background. Technician's gloved hand visible at edge of frame
holding a dial indicator.

### 3. Brinelling marks on shaft journal
Close-up of a polished steel shaft journal surface from a centrifugal compressor, showing
three distinct brinelling indentations arranged in a line, each approximately 3mm diameter.
The shaft is clamped in a V-block on an inspection surface. Magnifying lamp positioned
above. Background shows a calibrated micrometer and bearing wear measurement chart.
Daylight-balanced industrial lighting, high contrast, sharp focus on indentation detail.

## Seal & Gasket Failures

### 4. Leaking mechanical seal on pump housing
Eye-level photograph of a centrifugal pump casing with visible oil weeping from the
mechanical seal housing. A dark amber oil streak runs down the grey painted cast-iron
surface approximately 15cm long. The seal flush tubing is connected and shows condensation
droplets. Industrial pump room background with pipe racks and valve wheels. Warm
fluorescent lighting mixed with overhead high-bay LEDs. Water droplets on floor below
the leak point.

### 5. Blown gasket with carbon deposits
Top-down photograph of a disassembled flange joint from a compressor intercooler. The
spiral-wound gasket is visibly distorted with a 40mm section that has blown through,
leaving a black carbon deposit ring on both mating surfaces. The stainless steel inner
winding is torn and partially missing. Surrounding bolts show heat tinting. Parts laid on
brown kraft paper on a workbench. Natural daylight from a nearby window, sharp detail.

### 6. Cracked seal O-ring
Macro photograph of a viton O-ring removed from a hydraulic actuator. The O-ring shows
a circumferential crack at the 3-o'clock position with visible hardening and loss of
elasticity across its entire surface. A caliper jaw is positioned behind the ring showing
75mm OD. Background: a clean grey ESD mat with a seal kit package. Ring light illumination,
no shadows, clinical inspection lighting.

## Corrosion & Erosion

### 7. Internal pipe corrosion with scale buildup
Photograph taken inside a 6-inch cooling water pipe using a borescope-style perspective.
The pipe interior shows heavy tuberculation with rust-orange and dark brown nodules
covering 70% of the visible surface area. A measurement scale sticker is visible on the
pipe end. The pipe has been cut open for inspection, resting on concrete plant floor.
Harsh LED work light from the side creating deep shadows in the corrosion pits.

### 8. Impeller erosion and pitting
Photograph of a removed centrifugal pump impeller sitting on a wooden pallet. The
impeller vanes show extensive leading-edge erosion with irregular pitting patterns,
material loss visible on at least 6 of the 8 vanes. Some vanes have through-holes near
the tip. The hub shows minor corrosion. Industrial workshop background with overhead
crane visible. Even diffused lighting, high resolution showing metal grain texture at
erosion boundaries.

### 9. Galvanic corrosion at dissimilar metal joint
Close-up photograph of a pipe-to-flange transition where an aluminium flange is bolted
to a steel pipe. White aluminium oxide deposits surround all four visible bolt holes.
The steel side shows minor orange rust staining. The galvanic corrosion zone extends
approximately 20mm from the joint line. Clear labels visible on both components.
Shot on inspection bench with calibration gauge in background. Ring flash, even lighting.

## Electrical Faults

### 10. Burnt motor terminal with melted insulation
Photograph of an open electric motor terminal box. Three phase terminals visible; the
centre terminal (Phase B) shows severe overheating with melted black insulation sleeving,
discoloured copper conductor (blue-green-black gradient), and soot deposits on the
interior of the terminal box cover. The other two terminals appear normal. A fluke-style
multimeter probe is visible at the edge. Industrial motor room background, LED work
light illuminating the terminal box directly.

### 11. Arc flash damage on contactor
Top-down photograph of a disassembled contactor from a motor control centre. The
contact surfaces show heavy arc erosion with pitted and blackened copper contacts, a
small melted crater on the moving contact arm, and soot patterns radiating outward on
the white plastic housing. Broken spring fragment visible. Components laid on an
anti-static tray. Clinical overhead lighting, forensic documentation style.

### 12. Cable insulation breakdown
Close-up of a 3-core industrial power cable with the outer sheath stripped back. The
insulation on the red phase conductor shows a 15mm perforation with carbon tracking
along the insulation surface. Brown discolouration extends 40mm from the fault point.
The other two conductors show minor thermal stress. Cable clamped in a vise on a
workbench. Magnification ring light, high detail on insulation texture and carbon tracks.

## Weld & Structural Defects

### 13. Weld undercut on pressure vessel nozzle
Photograph of a circumferential weld joining a nozzle to a pressure vessel shell. The
weld toe on the vessel side shows a visible undercut groove approximately 1.5mm deep
and 8mm long, clearly illuminated by a dye penetrant inspection (DPI) with red
developer contrasting against the white background. Inspector's gloved hand holding a
weld gauge nearby. Industrial fabrication shop background, overhead crane visible.

### 14. Fatigue crack in structural beam
Photograph of a painted steel I-beam support bracket at a plant structure connection
point. A 60mm fatigue crack is visible emanating from the weld toe, with hairline
branching cracks. The paint has cracked along the main crack line, showing bare rusted
metal beneath. An ultrasonic thickness gauge probe is positioned on the beam web.
Outdoor structural steelwork, overcast natural light, industrial plant visible in
background. Sharp focus on crack detail.

### 15. Porosity in submerged arc weld
Radiograph-style photograph (backlit) of a submerged arc weld on a 25mm steel plate.
Multiple spherical gas pores of 1-4mm diameter are visible in a linear cluster along
the weld centreline. The radiograph shows the characteristic white-on-dark contrast.
Calibration step wedge visible at edge of image. Clean laboratory environment, even
backlighting, technical documentation style.

## Thermal & Insulation Defects

### 16. Thermal image of overheated electrical panel
Thermographic image of an electrical distribution board showing a 45°C temperature
anomaly on the top-right breaker position. The thermal image uses iron-bow palette
with the hotspot clearly visible in white-yellow against a blue-purple background.
The visible light overlay shows the breaker panel with circuit labels. A reference
temperature tag is visible on an adjacent panel. The image has embedded temperature
scale bar (25°C to 55°C range). Industrial electrical room environment.

### 17. Damaged insulation on steam pipe
Photograph of a section of 4-inch steam pipe where the aluminium cladding has been
punctured and the mineral wool insulation beneath is wet, compressed, and partially
missing. The exposed pipe surface shows orange rust staining and mineral deposit
streaks. Condensation droplets hang from the torn insulation edge. Surrounding
pipe insulation appears intact with proper aluminium jacketing. Plant mechanical
room, overhead lighting, pipes and valves in background.

### 18. Refractory lining spalling in furnace
Photograph looking into a small industrial furnace through the inspection port. The
refractory lining shows a 200mm section where the hot-face brick has spalled away,
exposing the insulating backup layer beneath. Fallen refractory debris visible on the
furnace floor. The remaining brick surface shows thermal stress cracking. Bright
furnace interior lighting contrasted with dark exterior. An inspection tag is visible
on the furnace door frame.

## Fluid System Defects

### 19. Hydraulic oil contamination sample
Photograph of a clear glass sample bottle containing hydraulic oil that is visibly
contaminated — the oil is dark amber-grey instead of the normal clear honey colour,
with visible fine metallic particles suspended throughout. The bottle has a label
showing "COMP-02 Sump, sampled 2026-07-15". The sample sits on a white surface next
to a clean oil sample for comparison. A particle count certificate is partially visible
in the background. Even diffused lighting, no reflections on glass.

### 20. Steam trap failure with condensate pooling
Photograph of a failed steam trap on a 2-inch condensate line. The trap body shows
mineral deposits around the inlet connection. A bucket is positioned below catching
a steady drip of hot condensate (steam visible rising from the bucket). The trap's
temperature gauge reads 95°C on the inlet side and 45°C on the outlet side,
indicating it is passing condensate without properly trapping steam. Industrial
plant piping environment, warm lighting.

### 21. Coolant leak with mineral deposits
Photograph of a cooling water header pipe connection where a slow weep has created
a white-blue mineral deposit buildup (calcium carbonate scale) approximately 30mm
long hanging from the pipe joint. The deposit formation has a stalactite-like shape.
A puddle of water with similar mineral residue is on the concrete floor below. The
pipe insulation nearby shows water damage staining. Mechanical room environment with
overhead LED lighting.

## Mechanical Component Defects

### 22. Worn gear teeth with pitting
Close-up photograph of two meshing spur gears removed from a gearbox. The drive gear
shows surface pitting on at least 8 tooth flanks, concentrated on the pitch line
region. The driven gear shows scoring marks and a polished wear band. The gear set
is held at an angle to show tooth profiles. A gear tooth profile gauge is visible
in the background. Workshop bench, overhead fluorescent lighting, metallic
reflections carefully controlled.

### 23. Bent pump shaft with runout
Photograph of a centrifugal pump shaft supported on V-blocks with a dial indicator
resting on the shaft surface at the bearing journal. The indicator needle shows
0.35mm total indicated runout (TIR), well above the 0.05mm tolerance visible on a
specification sheet pinned to the wall behind. The shaft has visible scoring at the
seal area and minor corrosion on the keyway. Workshop inspection area, even
lighting, sharp focus on the dial indicator face.

### 24. Cracked foundation bolt
Photograph of a concrete equipment foundation with a cast-in anchor bolt. The bolt
shows a circumferential crack at the base where it emerges from the concrete pad,
with a slight angular misalignment suggesting the bolt has partially yielded. A
crack width gauge is held next to the crack showing 2.1mm. The concrete pad surface
shows minor oil staining. Plant floor environment, overhead lighting with the
foundation pad well-illuminated from two angles to eliminate shadows in the crack.

### 25. Coupling wear and misalignment evidence
Photograph of a flexible coupling (jaw type) removed from between a motor and pump.
The elastomer spider insert shows three of six jaws with chunk material missing and
heat-blackened edges. The metal coupling hubs show scoring marks from metal-to-metal
contact. A straightedge and feeler gauge set are positioned nearby, and the
measurement sheet shows 0.8mm angular misalignment recorded. Workshop bench,
overhead lighting, components arranged for clear visual documentation.
